## Design Context

### Users
One performer (the repo owner) driving `ddj200-decks.pd` through the DDJ-200 web page (`ddj-web/`). The same page gets used in three ways: live on the laptop next to the controller in a dim room, where it has to be readable at a glance while the hands stay on the hardware; as a touch remote on a phone or tablet over Wi-Fi; and for practice and setup at home (loading songs, setting cues, calibrating).

### Brand Personality
Hardware-faithful with a subtle Pulsar Interactive feel. It should read like an extension of the DDJ-200: physical, utilitarian, labelled the way the controller is. Pulsar brand cues stay understated, never decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark theme only (dim venues).
- Deck A = cyan, deck B = magenta. These colors match the controller and light show and must not change.
- Controller vocabulary (CUE, PLAY, SYNC, pads) over app vocabulary.
- Anti-references: generic dark dashboard, neon glow for its own sake, card soup.

### Design Principles
1. Glanceable first: the playing state, time and position of each deck can be read from a distance.
2. Mirror the hardware: layout, labels and colors follow the DDJ-200, so the web page and the controller share one mental model.
3. Touch is first class: every control works with a finger on a phone. No hover-only or right-click-only actions.
4. Live safety: anything that interrupts audio (eject, load, seek) is hard to trigger by accident mid-set.
5. Subtle brand: a Pulsar touch in the details, not in the chrome.
